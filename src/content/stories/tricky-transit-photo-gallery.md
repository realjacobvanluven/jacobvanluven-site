The SkyTrain approaches the deck at Scott Road station in Surrey, B.C. Surrey residents must get creative with how they get around town; especially if they don't have cars.

### Scott Road SkyTrain Station

<figure class="media-placeholder" data-kind="image" data-file="tricky-transit-photo-gallery/02-scott-road-skytrain-station">
  <p>Image placeholder: Scott Road SkyTrain Station (original file: IMG_2772_edited.jpg)</p>
</figure>

One of four SkyTrain stations in Surrey, Scott Road station is a constant buzzing hub of transit riders going to and from Vancouver, Burnaby and New Westminster.

### Park & Ride

<figure class="media-placeholder" data-kind="image" data-file="tricky-transit-photo-gallery/03-park-ride">
  <p>Image placeholder: Park & Ride (original file: IMG_2745.jpg)</p>

The front rows of the park and ride parking lot at Scott Road station in Surrey, B.C. are often full during the day from SkyTrain commuters who forgo the bus.

### KPU students opting for bus travel

<figure class="media-placeholder" data-kind="image" data-file="tricky-transit-photo-gallery/04-kpu-students-opting-for-bus-travel">
  <p>Image placeholder: KPU students opting for bus travel (original file: IMG_2728.jpg)</p>
</figure>

Students at Kwantlen Polytechnic University's Surrey campus often wait in line to cram into full busses as the large school is not near any SkyTrain stations.
