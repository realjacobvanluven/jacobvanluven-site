By analyzing data published on [metrovancouver.org](http://www.metrovancouver.org/metro2040/sustainable-transportation/vehicle-movement/vehicle-km-travelled/Pages/explore-vkt-per-capita-data.aspx), we are able to visualize which Metro Vancouver residents have the lengthiest vehicle commutes. Not surprisingly, Maple Ridge residents travel the farthest by car. Township of Langley residents travel the farthest by car out of all municipalities in Metro Vancouver with a population over 100,000.

Using data taken from the same dataset, we are also able to visualize where residents are travelling the farthest by transit. Langley Township residents travel the farthest by transit on average, followed by White Rock and Delta residents.
