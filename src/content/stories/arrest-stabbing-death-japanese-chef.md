[The Vancouver Police Department have made an arrest](https://vpd.ca/news/2024/06/12/vpd-makes-arrest-in-murder-of-japanese-citizen/) following a six day investigation into the stabbing death of a renowned Japanese-national chef.

Timothy Isborn, 32, is charged with second degree murder in connection to the death of 32-year-old Wataru Kakiuchi.

Shortly before 3:30 AM on June 5th, Kakiuchi was found with stab wounds near Main St. and Union St. Police do not believe that Kakiuchi and Isborn had previously known each other.

Kakiuchi was a longtime chef at Hapa Izakaya, a popular Japanese restaurant in Yaletown.

Justin Ault, owner of Hapa Izakaya, told [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/10562816/wataru-kakiuchi-japanese-chef-chinatown-suspect-arrested/) that Kakiuchi was out with co-workers in Gastown on the night of his death.

If you have information regarding this ongoing case, contact the VPD Homicide Unit at 604-717-2500.
