The trial for three Surrey men charged with the 2022 murder of Arnold and Joanne De Jong has been delayed by one year.

The trial of Gurkaran Singh, Abhijeet Singh and Kushveer Toor was supposed to begin on May 27, 2024. Jury selection has now been moved to May 14, 2025, and the trial is meant to begin on May 20, 2025.

All proceedings are expected to occur at the B.C. Supreme Court in Abbotsford.

The bodies of Arnold, 77, and Joanne, 76, were found on May 9, 2022 inside their Abbotsford home on the 33600 block of Arcadian Way.

Abbotsford Police and the [Integrated Homicide Investigation Team](http://www.homicideteam.ca/) announced charges against the accused for the couple's murder on Dec. 16, 2022. All men were in their early-twenties at the time of their arrest and none of them had prior criminal records.

“We have never heard of them. We do not know them, had never seen them,” said daughter Kimberly Coleman in an [interview with CTV. ](https://bc.ctvnews.ca/family-speaks-out-1-year-after-abbotsford-b-c-seniors-murdered-in-their-home-1.6391415)

The motive behind the couple’s murder continues to remain a mystery. IHIT spokesperson Sgt. Pierotti announced at a press conference that only one of the men had known the victims prior to the murder.

The couple owned their own trucking company, De Jong Transport, where Arnold personally traveled almost $2 million miles transporting produce, eggs and flowers.

Arnold and Joanne leave behind grandchildren and three daughters.

“They were great parents,” said Coleman.
