The Preparedness Series promotes resilience in First Nations communities by fostering emergency readiness through focused discussions on emergencies and disasters. I piloted and developed the first four instalments of the series on [forest fires](https://www.emergencyplanningsecretariat.com/forest-fires), extreme heat, [drought](https://www.emergencyplanningsecretariat.com/drought) and [evacuations](https://www.emergencyplanningsecretariat.com/preparing-for-evacuation).
