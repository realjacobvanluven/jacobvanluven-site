The Prince George RCMP is asking for the public's assistance in locating a 38-year-old wanted man.

[Jesse John Braaten is wanted for multiple charges](https://bc-cb.rcmp-grc.gc.ca/ViewPage.action?siteNodeId=2087&languageId=1&contentId=84416) including assault, extortion and three counts of assault with a weapon.

Braaten is described as a six-foot-three caucasian male, weighing 221 pounds. He has blue eyes and balding blonde hair.

Braaten is considered dangerous and should not be approached. If located, contact police immediately.

If you have any information about Braaten’s whereabouts, contact the Prince George RCMP at 250-561-3300. If you wish to remain anonymous, you can contact Crime Stoppers at 1-800-222-8477 (TIPS).
