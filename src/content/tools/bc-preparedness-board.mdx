This board pulls the public BC and Environment Canada data feeds into one place, sorted with the most serious items first. It checks the sources each time you open it, and falls back to a saved copy (shown with its age) if a source is down.

**It is a portfolio project, not an emergency service.** For decisions about your safety, use the official sources linked at the bottom of the page.
