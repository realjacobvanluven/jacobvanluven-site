Most workplace writing isn't unclear because the ideas are hard. It's unclear because of the wrapping. This checker looks for the usual culprits: inflated phrases, passive voice, very long sentences, filler words and acronyms nobody defined. It also gives a reading-grade estimate.

It is a set of plain rules, not AI: the same text always gets the same result, and every flag says why. Treat it as a second pair of eyes, not a verdict. Rules are written for general business writing, so technical terms you can't avoid are fine.
