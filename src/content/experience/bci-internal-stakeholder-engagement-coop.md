I had the privilege of completing two co-op terms at the [British Columbia Investment Management Corporation (BCI)](https://www.bci.ca/) headquarters in Victoria, B.C. During this time, I worked closely with key internal stakeholders to develop impactful and strategic communications. Although confidentiality restrictions prevent me from sharing specific deliverables, the experience significantly enhanced my ability to convert high-level subject matter into easy-to-understand content.

Developed compelling messaging and implemented internal communication strategies that were informal, engaging, and aligned with the company’s culture and purpose. This included drafting and executing news articles, and designing and managing content for the company intranet to boost employee engagement and foster a connected, informed workplace.
