The Surrey RCMP is asking for the public's assistance in [locating 27-year-old Riley Lafontaine](https://bc-cb.rcmp-grc.gc.ca/ViewPage.action?siteNodeId=2126&languageId=1&contentId=84128) of Surrey, B.C.

Lafontaine is facing charges in jurisdictions policed by the Surrey RCMP, Delta Police Department and the BC Transit Police. His charges include multiple counts of breaching release orders, breaking and entering and failing to comply with probation orders.

He is described as a Caucasian man with a slender build. He stands 5-foot-11, and weighs around 150 pounds. He has short brown hair and blue eyes.

Lafontaine has specific tattoos including a wolf tattoo on the front of his neck, teardrops under both eyes and a crucifix tattoo on his forehead. He also has multiple other tattoos on his hands and arms.

If you see Lafontaine, the Surrey RCMP advises you to not approach him and call the police immediately.

If you have information on Lafontaine’s whereabouts, please contact Crime Stoppers or the Surrey RCMP at 604-599-0502. To report anonymously, please call 1-800-222-8477 or visit [www.solvecrime.ca](http://www.solvecrime.ca).
