\*THIS STORY WAS WRITTEN FOR JRNL 4900 UNDER A THREE-HOUR DEADLINE\*

The Surrey RCMP responded to a major accident yesterday near the Semiahmoo Shopping Center that left a 60-year-old woman with serious injuries.

The accident occurred on the corner of Martin Dr. and Southmere Cres. at around 2:00 PM. All traffic along Martin Dr., north of 16 Avenue and south of Southmere Cres., was halted for the investigation. By 6:00 PM, the RCMP announced the re-opening of the roads.

The driver remained on scene and is cooperating with authorities.

If you have any information about this investigation, contact the Surrey RCMP non-emergency line at 604-599-0502 and quote file number 2024-84295.

This area has a long documented history of pedestrian strikes, dating all the way back to 2013 when a 45-year-old woman was hit crossing Martin Dr. at 16 Avenue.

On May 16, Surrey RCMP responded to a hit-and-run of an elderly female pedestrian at the exact same intersection as yesterday's collision.
