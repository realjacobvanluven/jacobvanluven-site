Sherrie Budai is a nurse from Surrey, B.C who commuted daily from her home in Newton to her workplace in East Vancouver for over ten years by means of bus, SkyTrain and walking.

### Where would you depart from and where were you going?

So I would depart from the bus stop on Scott Road and Wade, and the endpoint would be the [Main and Terminal Skytrain station](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/SB+Scott+Rd+@+Wade+Rd,+Delta,+BC+V4E+3K7/Main+Street-Science+World,+Vancouver,+BC/@49.1968588,-123.0661358,12z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m13!4m12!1m5!1m1!1s0x5485dbe47aaf3e73:0x8c1147c25be50fce!2m2!1d-122.8905559!2d49.123827!1m5!1m1!1s0x5486716418ba8c37:0x5cfe76622b9771ea!2m2!1d-123.10035!2d49.273172) and then I'd walk from there to work.

### 2. How long on average would you spend waiting for the bus or the train?

[On the way there] if the bus was on time, it would be about five minutes because I would time it right. On the way back, it could be anywhere from five minutes to like 20 minutes or longer.

### 3. What time would you leave home in order to arrive on time?

I'd have to leave the house at 20 after five in the morning

### 4. Describe the route you took, including the buses/trains:

To get from the house to the bus stop I would walk, it would take about five minutes. Then I'd take the 340 bus to the [22nd Street Skytrain station](https://www.google.com/maps/place/22nd+Street+Station/@49.1999706,-122.9512312,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x5485d88237ea5a57:0x209d3383928f8ece!8m2!3d49.1999133!4d-122.9492655)… and then from there, it would be about like between 30 and 40 minutes on the train and then another 20 minute walk.

### 5. Would you take this same route home?

Yeah in reverse.

### 6. What was your least favorite part about this daily trip?

I think my least favorite would be the frustration, like the bus would frequently be canceled. I mean, if it showed up on time, that was great but it would be canceled. I signed up for alerts and a lot of times you didn't get the alert, you would get it like half an hour after.

### 7. How frequently did you encounter delays while en-route?

At least once a week.

### 8. On average, how long would you spend commuting each day?

On the way in, it was about an hour and 15. On the way back it would be an hour 50, sometimes two hours.

### 9. How much would your commute cost you each month?

I think it was about like $40 a week I paid so multiply that by four [that’s] $120 a month.

### 10. Why did you choose to take transit instead of other options like driving or Uber?

Uber was too expensive. [My workplace] wouldn't give me a parking spot that I didn't have to pay for so I'd have to pay like 90 bucks a month to park where I work.

### 11. How would you commute for other trips like running errands or going out?

I drive or I walk.

### 12. Would you say Surrey’s public transit has improved, gotten worse or remained the same since you began commuting downtown for work?

I think that it's gotten better, however the amount of ridership has increased. I think they've added in more routes and they've added in more frequency, but it hasn't managed to keep up with the amount of people, especially the amount of people that commute from Surrey. And so while they might have made improvements, I think the overall experience has gotten worse in my opinion.
